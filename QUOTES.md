# Quotes

Quotations that the book uses or may use. Each one is exact, in its original language, with its
source and the status of the check (AGENTS.md: never invent a reference). The rest of the content
comes in the next ingest.

The first quotation is the one that [h0ffmann/picasso](https://github.com/h0ffmann/picasso), the
owner's Nix labs, carries in its credits, in the name of the great Pablo Picasso: people say an
artist got lucky, and the answer is that the luck found him at work (Hoffmann, 13 October 2026).
