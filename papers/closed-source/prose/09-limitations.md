# Limitations {#sec:limits}

I state the limits of my argument plainly, in line with the Ouroboric value of honesty about
results.

- **Position, not measurement.** This paper argues from cited evidence; it reports no new
  experiment. Its claims about the cost of reverse engineering rest on the studies cited, not on
  my own measurements.
- **Allegations, not findings.** The Apple v. OpenAI dispute and the distillation accusations are
  claims by interested parties. No court or independent body had ruled on them at the time of
  writing.
- **Secondary sources for 2026 events.** I relied on news reports and one law-firm summary for
  the 2026 events. The court docket was not retrieved directly, and secondary sources disagree on
  the judge's suffix of the case number.
- **Not all closedness is wrong.** Patient data, personal data, security-sensitive details and
  material under embargo have good reasons to stay closed. My argument concerns methods, code
  and models whose secrecy protects only their owner.
- **Author's interest.** I build open-source tools and cite my own projects. Readers
  should weigh Section [-@sec:related] accordingly.
- **Not yet peer reviewed.** This is a v0 preprint.
