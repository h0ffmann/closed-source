# Limitations {#sec:limits}

We state the limits of our argument clearly. This agrees with the Ouroboric value of honesty about
results.

- **Position, not measurement.** This paper uses cited evidence for its argument. It does not
  report a new experiment. Its claims about the cost of reverse engineering come from the cited
  studies, not from our own measurements.
- **Allegations, not findings.** The Apple v. OpenAI dispute and the distillation accusations are
  claims by interested parties. When we wrote this paper, there was no decision about them from a
  court or an independent body.
- **Secondary sources for 2026 events.** For the 2026 events, our sources are news reports and one
  summary from a law firm. We did not fetch the court docket directly. Secondary sources do not
  agree about the judge suffix of the case number.
- **Not all closedness is wrong.** Patient data, personal data, security-sensitive details and
  material under embargo have good reasons to stay closed. Our argument is about methods, code and
  models whose secrecy protects only their owner.
- **Authors' interest.** The authors build open-source tools and cite their own projects. Readers
  should consider this interest when they evaluate Section [-@sec:related].
- **Not yet peer reviewed.** This is a v0 preprint.
