# Private code and private science lose their value

My position, as I said it to friend3: in the long term, private code has no commercial value, and
private science has none either. With agents, two things grow very fast: reverse engineering and
science. Somebody will reverse engineer your papers, and everybody else's. The time of making
money by keeping software shut is ending soon. That is why I now make only open work.

I gave the same warning to a colleague who planned to write code for a contract with a large
company, all of it sealed.

**Status: open question.** This is a forecast and my own position, not a fact with a source.
What would support it: a recorded case where agents rebuilt a closed product or a paper's
method, and a measure of what the owner lost.
