# Brazilian instruments under technical cooperation agreements

The truth for [[202610122357-marola-forecaster-contextual-bandit]] is Brazilian: open Brazilian
data, plus private data reached through technical cooperation agreements (Acordos de Cooperação
Técnica, ACTs) with the Brazilian Navy, Petrobras and INPO.

The score is only as good as its truth. Operational WeatherBench checks each model against
analyses and ERA5 ([[202610122356-operational-weatherbench]]); Marola checks against buoys and
instruments on the Brazilian coast, which no global benchmark covers.

**Open:** which ACTs are signed and which are planned; what each lets us publish (scores only, or
data too); and "INPO": is this the institute's name as written, or another acronym?
