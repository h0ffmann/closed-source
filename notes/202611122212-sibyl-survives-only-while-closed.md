# Sibyl survives only while it is closed

**The facts (verified in [[202611122211-sibyl-is-a-closed-judge]]).** Sibyl judges every citizen,
no citizen may read how, and Sibyl says its true form is secret "out of consideration for public
opinion".

**[ANALYSIS] The claim.** Sibyl is closed source in the paper's broad sense: a judge whose code no
one may inspect. Its authority does not come from being right; it comes from being unread. The day
the public reads the source, the verdicts lose their force. That is the paper's thesis turned into
a story: closed source is a protection only while nobody looks.

**[ANALYSIS] Hard to keep closed from inside.** The paper says closed source "is not only easy to
read from outside; it is hard to keep closed from inside" (`papers/closed-source/prose/01-introduction.md`).
In episode 16 outsiders walk into the core, and in episode 17 the system shows itself to the one
man it cannot measure. The secret leaks to exactly the people it was hidden from.

**[ANALYSIS] Who judges the judge.** Sibyl is run by the criminally asymptomatic, the people it cannot measure, so no
one outside can say how good the judge is. This is the MetaQuality question
([[202610131752-what-is-metaquality]]) with no answer allowed.
