# Marcus Baffa laughed with me

Marcus Baffa is thinker 46 of [`50THINKERS.md`](../50THINKERS.md), where his reason is still "to
be explained". This note is a part of the explanation. Everything in it is my memory; nothing
was checked against an outside source.

- Marcus Baffa was a technical leader at Stone. Everyone praised him.
- I will never forget my first contact with him. It was the last interview before I was hired.
- I told him that I was taking Latin classes at UFRJ. At that time nobody understood why I was
  doing that, and I did not understand it myself. It is one of my best memories of UFRJ.
- He laughed. For years I told it as "he laughed at me".
- Over time I saw that Baffa was always with me. He was laughing *with* me. The correction is
  Mr. Nobody's: [[202610132354-the-preposition-makes-the-difference]].
- Baffa loved Scala and Erlang. I will go on writing Scala, and my second brain stands on
  Erlang, through Gleam.
- He passed away. I have missed him very much over the years.
