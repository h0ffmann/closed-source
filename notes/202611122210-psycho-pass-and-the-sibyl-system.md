# Psycho-Pass and the Sibyl System

*Psycho-Pass* is a Japanese anime series, "produced by Production I.G", "written by Gen Urobuchi"
and "co-directed by Naoyoshi Shiotani and Katsuyuki Motohiro". Its first season has 22 episodes
and aired "between October 12, 2012, and March 22, 2013" on Fuji TV's Noitamina block (Wikipedia,
*Psycho-Pass*).

In the series, Japan is run by the Sibyl System, "a powerful biomechatronic computer network which
continually monitors the psychological traits of Japanese citizens". Each citizen has a
Psycho-Pass with a Crime Coefficient; when it "exceeds the accepted threshold (100), they are
pursued". The police carry the Dominator, a gun that "can only fire when approved by the Sibyl
System and triggered by its owner" (Wikipedia, *Psycho-Pass*).

Wikipedia names its influences: "Urobuchi insisted on using a Philip K. Dick-inspired, dystopian
narrative", and Shiotani cited *Blade Runner*, *Minority Report*, *Gattaca* and *Brazil*.
