# Sibyl is a closed judge: brains in a tower, kept secret

**The public story.** Citizens are told that a computer network judges them
([[202611122210-psycho-pass-and-the-sibyl-system]]).

**What it is (episodes 16 and 17).** In episode 16, "The Gate to Judgment" (7 February 2013),
an intruder reaches the core under the NONA Tower and says: "This is... the true form of the Sibyl
System!" In episode 17, "Iron Heart" (14 February 2013), Makishima learns that Sibyl is a
"supercomputer network composed of the brains of criminally-asymptomatic individuals" like himself,
and he is invited "to become part of the system"; he refuses (Wikipedia, *Psycho-Pass season 1*).
The fan wiki gives "247 known members in total" (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *Sibyl System*).

**Why it is closed (episode 22).** Sibyl says it itself: "The true form of the Sibyl System is
currently kept secret out of consideration for public opinion" (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *0122 Perfect
World*, 22 March 2013).

**Who judges the judges.** The members are the people the system cannot read: the criminally
asymptomatic, "individuals" like Makishima (Wikipedia, *Psycho-Pass season 1*, episode 17). The
fan wiki puts it the same way: "a hive mind made up of people who, like Shogo Makishima, are
criminally asymptomatic" (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *Sibyl System*).
