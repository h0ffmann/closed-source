# Akane reads the source and keeps it closed

**The facts.** In episode 20, "The Place Where Justice Is Found" (7 March 2013), Sibyl reveals
itself to Inspector Akane Tsunemori; she "comes to understand why the system is necessary, though
not whether it is justifiable" (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *0120*). A review gives her reason for silence
as "Japan would collapse if Sibyl were to disappear right now" (Angry Anime Bitches, episode 20
review; a paraphrase, not her line).

In episode 22, "Perfect World" (22 March 2013), she tells Sibyl: "You shouldn't underestimate
humans. We're always aiming for a better society. One day, someone will come to this room to turn
off the power." And: "The law doesn't protect people. People protect the law" (Psycho-Pass Wiki,
*0122 Perfect World*; Wikipedia gives the first as "someone will pull their plug in the future").

**[ANALYSIS] The best case for closure.** Akane is the strongest argument against the paper,
because she is honest. She read the source, and she still chose not to publish it, because the
society runs on it and has nothing to replace it with. Opening it today would break more than it
fixes.

**[ANALYSIS] Why it does not save closed source.** Her promise is that closure is temporary:
someone will turn it off. She does not defend the secret; she buys time. The paper can take her as
the moment before release, not as a reason never to release. "People protect the law" is the open
source position in five words: the rule is only as good as the people who can read it and change
it ([[202610181354-open-source-first-for-science-after-pirsig]]).
