# Why I wrote fast, and from preprint to book

What I told friend3 about the state of the work, each point with what the record shows on 13
October 2026.

- **It is published, and it went from a preprint to a book.** Verified. The latest version is
  v0.3.0, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23349831](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23349831); Zenodo's
  record gives its type as "Book" (<https://zenodo.org/records/23349831>). The change is in
  [`RELEASES.md`](../RELEASES.md), "v0.3.0: what changed". The DOI that always opens the newest
  version is [10.5281/zenodo.23333656](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23333656).
- **No review.** I thought of sending it to a group at UFSC, but I know of nobody there who
  reviews this. Unverified: it is what I know, not a search.
- **Why I ran to write it.** Everybody talks about one subject now: reverse engineering with
  agents. The video I sent is "REA: A ferramenta que está mudando a engenharia reversa", on
  Leonardo Bissoli's channel (<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQZ9XfNqR7c>). The title and the
  channel were read from YouTube. The video was not watched, so this note does not say what it
  argues. "Everybody" is my impression.
- **The paper is not good yet, and I know it.** It came out of my own paper factory, Paper
  Fabric ([how it makes a paper](../docs/4-ways-of-working/paper-fabric.md)), so I can change it
  when I want.
- **Indexed together with articles from Nature.** Partial. What the record shows is narrower:
  the book's record names two articles of Nature Portfolio as works it cites, and DataCite
  holds those ties: [10.1038/nature10836](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10836) (*Nature*, 2012)
  and [10.1038/s41597-022-01710-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01710-x) (*Scientific
  Data*, 2022). [`RELATED_WORK.md`](../RELATED_WORK.md) has the reading and its date. If I
  meant a search result where the book stood beside Nature's articles, that needs the name of
  the index, the link and the date.
