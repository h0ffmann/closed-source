# Policy: Ouroboric ideas, reptilians and the eternal cycle

The owner's word, as it came: **the Ouroboros is a myth.** From that, a policy for this book:

1. **Ouroboric ideas are metaphor.** Ouroboric names its self-improving factories after the
   serpent that eats its own tail (OUROBORIC.md, §1). The book uses the serpent as a picture of a
   feedback loop, never as a claim about the world. See [[202610150158-ouroboros-the-myth]].
2. **Reptilians are a conspiracy theory, not a fact.** The book may tell where the idea came from
   (occultism, pulp fiction, David Icke), but never states or hints that reptilians exist. See
   [[202610150159-reptilians-from-occultism-to-conspiracy]].
3. **The eternal cycle is an idea to weigh, not a law.** Eternal return is a philosophical and
   religious idea; the book treats it as a thought experiment, as Nietzsche did. See
   [[202610150200-the-eternal-cycle]].
4. **Myth is data about the mind.** Why people tell the same myth again and again is a fair
   question; the book asks it with Jung, see [[202610150201-why-jung-used-dreams-and-myths]].
5. **Amendment (2026-10-15): is a myth viral?** Religion as a virus of the mind, after Rust
   Cohle and Dawkins, and whether seeding Ouroboric ideas spreads the same way. See
   [[202610150202-is-religion-like-a-virus-is-ouroboros-seeding-viral]].
6. Every factual claim about these myths follows rule 7 of SMART_NOTE_POLICY.md.
