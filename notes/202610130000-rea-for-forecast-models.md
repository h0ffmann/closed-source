# REA for forecast models

Ouroboric's REA pattern is Read, Evaluate, Adopt: read what others published, evaluate it on your
own terms, adopt only what passes. Picasso applies it to repositories (its `ouroboric-sharingan`
skill). Marola applies it to forecast models:

1. **Read** the method and the scores others publish
   ([[202610122356-operational-weatherbench]]).
2. **Evaluate** each model on Brazilian truth
   ([[202610122358-brazilian-instruments-under-acts]]).
3. **Adopt** the winner for operations, and keep the rest in simulation
   ([[202610122359-exploit-in-operations-explore-in-simulation]]).
