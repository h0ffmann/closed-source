# Is religion like a virus, as Rust Cohle says? Is Ouroboros seeding viral?

An amendment to [[202610150157-policy-ouroboric-ideas-reptilians-eternal-cycle]]. Two questions
of the owner.

**1. Is religion like a virus?** Rust Cohle (PERSONAS.md), the detective of *True Detective*
season 1 (2014), speaks of religion as something that spreads and infects. I could not fetch his
exact lines, so none is quoted here; the episode's transcript would verify it. The idea is older:
Richard Dawkins' essay "Viruses of the Mind" (1991) calls religious belief a mind virus, spread
by "epidemiology, not evidence," from parents and charismatic people. Alister McGrath answers that
ideas spread by choice, not by chance, and that no epidemic model has been shown to explain
religion.

**2. Is Ouroboros seeding viral?** Ouroboric seeds a company with a "brand seed" and a charter
(OUROBORIC.md, Asgard), and its factories feed what they learn back into themselves. If a seeded
idea copies itself into each new factory, it spreads like a meme. The difference I want to test:
a virus copies without asking, while the Ouroboric loop measures and adopts only what passes
(REA, [[202610130000-rea-for-forecast-models]]). Is evidence what turns a virus into a method?

**Open question.** Jung's answer would be the other side: a myth recurs because it is in the mind,
not because it infects it ([[202610150201-why-jung-used-dreams-and-myths]]).
