# The eternal cycle: eternal return from Pythagoras to Nietzsche

The idea that time is a loop and the same events come again, forever.

- **Pythagoreans.** Porphyry reports that "after certain specified periods, the same events occur
  again."
- **Stoics.** The world burns (*ekpyrosis*) and is born again (*palingenesis*), cycle after cycle.
- **Augustine** rejected it: it leaves no room for salvation or free will.
- **Nietzsche, 1882.** *The Gay Science* §341 asks how you would take a demon who says you must
  live "this life as you now live it and have lived it ... once more and innumerable times more."
  A test of how you live, more than a cosmology; scholars still argue which.

The Ouroboros is the picture of this loop ([[202610150158-ouroboros-the-myth]]).
