# Reptilians: from occultism and pulp fiction to conspiracy theory

A conspiracy theory with no evidence behind it. Its line, as the article tells it:

- **1888.** Helena Blavatsky's *The Secret Doctrine* speaks of "'dragon-men'" with a civilisation
  on Lemuria, a lost continent of Theosophy.
- **1929.** Robert E. Howard's story "The Shadow Kingdom" has "serpent men" who pass as humans.
- **1940s.** The occultist Maurice Doreal writes of a shape-changing "serpent race"; Michael Barkun
  thinks Doreal took it from Howard.
- **1999.** David Icke's *The Biggest Secret* makes it a theory of shape-shifting reptilians who
  rule humanity. Parts of QAnon later borrowed it, with antisemitic conspiracy elements.

The occult serpent here is the opposite of the Ouroboros: not a symbol of wholeness but a hidden
enemy ([[202610150158-ouroboros-the-myth]]).
