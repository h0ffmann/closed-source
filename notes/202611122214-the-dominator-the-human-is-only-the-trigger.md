# The Dominator: the human is only the trigger

**The facts.** The Dominator "can only fire when approved by the Sibyl System and triggered by its
owner" (Wikipedia, *Psycho-Pass*). In episode 1, "Crime Coefficient" (12 October 2012), a hostage's
Crime Coefficient climbs past 300 because she is terrified, and the Dominator in Kogami's hand
switches to its lethal mode. Akane cries "She's the victim here!" and paralyzes Kogami to stop him;
the hostage calms down and her reading drops (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *0101 Crime Coefficient*).

**[ANALYSIS] The claim.** The Dominator is the human in the loop as most systems build it: the
person holds the gun, the closed system decides. The human can only say no, and only by breaking
the tool. That is the risk with agents at scale, too
([[202610122239-espionage-and-agents-at-scale]]): an operator who approves verdicts they cannot read
is a trigger, not a judge.

**[ANALYSIS] Why the first episode matters.** The system reads fear as guilt, and no one can see
why it did. A verdict you can audit can be corrected; a verdict you cannot audit can only be
obeyed or disobeyed.
