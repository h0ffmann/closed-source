# Makishima's library: the reader the system cannot read

**The facts (fan wiki, with episodes where it gives them).** Shogo Makishima is "criminally
asymptomatic": "his Psycho-Pass remains unsullied" despite his crimes (episode 16). He names
Orwell's *Nineteen Eighty-Four* as a favourite (episode 4) and uses Foucault's *Discipline and
Punish* to name what bothers him about Sibyl (no episode given). He reads Shakespeare (episodes 6
and 8), and in episode 16 tells Kogami: "If you quoted Pascal, I would've said the same words to
you." His other authors include Nietzsche, Swift, Philip K. Dick, Ōe and Tanizaki. He believes
Sibyl turns citizens into "non-thinking automatons" (episode 14) (Psycho-Pass Wiki, *Shogo
Makishima*; *0116*).
