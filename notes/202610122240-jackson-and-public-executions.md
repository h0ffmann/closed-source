# Jackson's argument against summary executions

The owner asked to add "Samuel Jackson['s] argument to public executions".

**Open question: which Jackson?** My best reading is **Robert H. Jackson**, the US chief prosecutor
at Nuremberg, who argued for a public trial under law instead of the summary execution of the Nazi
leaders. His opening statement of 21 November 1945 is published by the Robert H. Jackson Center
([excerpts, PDF](https://www.roberthjackson.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Excerpts-from-the-Nuremberg-Opening-Statement.pdf));
not yet read here. If the owner meant someone else, change this note.
