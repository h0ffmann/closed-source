# "The magic you're looking for is in the work you're avoiding": who said it first

The line: "The magic you're looking for is in the work you're avoiding." The owner wanted it, and
its original poster.

**The earliest trace found.** A blog post dated 31 May 2019, "thought of the week: the magic
you're looking for is in the work you're avoiding"
(<https://keepupwithktap.com/2019/05/31/thought-of-the-week-the-magic-youre-looking-for-is-in-the-work-youre-avoiding/>).
It gives a longer form, as an image: "people romanticize their plans but dread the execution.
the magic you're looking for is in the work you're avoiding." Its writer says she saw it posted
by Nazanin Mandi "a few weeks back", and links to her Instagram profile.

**Later, and louder.** On X the line spread without a name: Chris Williamson on 27 August 2023
(<https://x.com/ChrisWillx/status/1695783230514069790>), then others in 2023 and 2024. A
newsletter gives it as "Anonymous" (TEDONA #154, <https://tedona.substack.com/p/tedona-154>).
A shop's poster credits a "Dipen Parmar"; no source backs that credit.

**What I make of it.** The best answer today: first seen in spring 2019, on Instagram, posted
by Nazanin Mandi, with the sentence before it that people now drop. Posted by her is not
written by her: she may have shared it from someone else.

**Status: partial.** The 2019 blog post and the X posts were found and read on 18 October 2026
(the blog through a fetch). The Instagram post itself was not found: Instagram needs a login,
and the blog links only to the profile. To verify: the Instagram post with its date, and a
search for the phrase before spring 2019. Until then, write "posted by Nazanin Mandi in 2019,
author unknown", never "Nazanin Mandi said".
