# The preposition makes the difference

"The preposition makes the difference." For years I said that a man laughed *at* me. He laughed
*with* me. The verb and the two people are the same in both sentences; one small word changes
who the other person is to me.

The same small word changes a sentence about work. To work **with** people, to work **for**
people and to take **from** people are three different things, and only the preposition tells
them apart.
