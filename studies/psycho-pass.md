# Psycho-Pass study: season 1, for the Closed Source paper

A plan to watch *Psycho-Pass* season 1 once, with the paper's question in hand, and to come out
with notes that can carry text. It builds on the six notes already in the slip box (the
"Psycho-Pass: the closed judge" section of [`notes/index.md`](../notes/index.md)).

## The question

Does the Sibyl System give the paper a clear case of closed source as a judge: a system whose
verdicts everyone obeys and whose code nobody may read? And what is the strongest answer against
the paper that the series gives? (The current answer: Akane, who reads the source and keeps it
closed.)

## How to watch

- **Which cut.** The original 22 episodes (Fuji TV, 12 October 2012 to 22 March 2013). Not the
  "new edit", which "divides the season into 11 episodes" and adds new footage (Wikipedia,
  *Psycho-Pass season 1*). Episode numbers in the notes follow the 22-episode cut.
- **Which release.** A licensed one with English subtitles. Wikipedia names Crunchyroll as the
  streamer in English-speaking regions, and Funimation's home video (11 March 2014) in North
  America. Write down which release and which subtitle track you used; fan wiki quotes can differ
  from it.
- **What to write down.** For each scene below: the episode, the time stamp, and the line as the
  licensed subtitle gives it. A quote goes into a note only with its time stamp.
- **One idea, one slip.** A new idea on the way is a new fleeting note (`/note`), not a line in this
  page.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Does Psycho-Pass go into the paper, or stay in the notes?** The paper's evidence is real
   cases; a work of fiction may fit better in an essay ([`DERIVATION.md`](../DERIVATION.md)) or as
   one framing paragraph.
2. **Is Akane the counter-case the paper must answer?** Note 202611122213 argues that she buys time
   rather than defends the secret. That reading waits for your approval.
3. **Season 1 only?** Later seasons and the films change what Sibyl is; this study leaves them out
   on purpose. Say if you want them.
4. **The author's slot.** Note 202611122213 has an `[AUTHOR ANECDOTE]` on whether you would make
   Akane's choice.
5. **Makishima's books.** Does any of them belong in `BOOKS.md`? Check each against the episodes
   first.

## After watching

- Write the new notes the guide marks "new note?" (episodes 3, 9 and 14), if they hold up.
- If the owner says yes to question 1, derive the paragraph or essay from the notes, and nothing
